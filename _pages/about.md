I am a remote sensing ecologist interested in understanding how polar ecosystems are changing and how to use satellite data to detect it. Currently, I am looking what optical and synthetic aperture radar satellite data can tell us about vegetation change on the Southern Ocean Islands, as part of [Securing Antarctica's Environmental Future](https://arcsaef.com/). 

In the past, I have used satellite data and water balance models to project climate change impacts on primary productivity in U.S. National Parks, [taught wildlife ecology](https://www.ecologyproject.org/) in Yellowstone National Park, monitored water quality (and measured tiny Mysis shrimp) at [Flathead Lake Biological Station](https://flbs.umt.edu/monitoring/lake-monitoring/), dug some deep snow pits on the [Juneau Icefield](https://www.juneauicefield.org/), and [evaluated and bias-corrected satellite precipitation data across Canada and Alaska](/publications). 

In my real life, I like to play ultimate, spend time outside, do crafts, and make art. I live in Brisbane and am a passionate former resident of Missoula and Seattle. This website is a work in progress and new content may be added soon...
